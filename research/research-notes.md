# User Research

## Problem

Cornell freshmen can feel overwhelmed by the club recruitment process. There are many organizations to choose from, but recruitment information and expectations are often unclear or spread across different platforms.

## Evidence

### Reddit Post 1
- Freshman stressed about competitive business/consulting recruitment
- Worried about missing career opportunities
- Concerned about workload
- Unsure whether selective clubs are worth it

### Reddit Post 2
- Freshman applied to 7-8 clubs
- Struggled with interviews
- Missed an application deadline
- Felt behind classmates
- Regretted not exploring less competitive/social clubs


## Key Pain Points

1. Club discovery
2. Understanding recruitment process
3. Tracking application deadlines
4. Preparing for interviews
5. Pressure surrounding competitive clubs
6. Finding clubs that match personal interests

## Product Opportunity

Build a platform that helps Cornell students:
- Discover clubs based on their interests and goals
- Understand each club's recruitment process
- Track deadlines and events
- Prepare for applications and interviews
- Find clubs based on fit rather than prestige alone
